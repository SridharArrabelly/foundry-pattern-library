# Wealth Management — Suitability & Advice Policy (demo excerpt)

## Suitability rule
Every investment recommendation must match the client's documented **risk profile**.
A **Conservative** client must not hold more than **70% equities**. Any portfolio that
breaches this threshold is **flagged as non-compliant** and requires a suitability review
before the next trade.

## Quarterly review
Relationship Managers must produce a **quarterly portfolio review** for each client,
covering performance vs. benchmark, any suitability breaches, and at least one
forward-looking recommendation.

## Product disclosures
Structured products must be accompanied by the current **Key Information Document (KID)**.
Recommendations without a valid KID on file are not permitted.

## Record keeping
All advice interactions are retained for **7 years** and are subject to audit.
